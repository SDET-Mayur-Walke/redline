# Contacting support

## Chat
The chat assistant in the app and on the website can track orders, start returns, cancel pending orders, change the
address on pending orders and answer questions from this help centre. Have your email address and order ID ready;
the assistant will ask for them before discussing an order.

## When a person takes over
The assistant raises a ticket for our support team when:
- a refund is above ₹5,000 and needs approval
- you report a missing parcel or a parcel marked delivered that you didn't receive
- you want to make a warranty claim
- you ask to speak to a person

Tickets are answered by email, usually within one business day. SaffronCart Plus members are answered first.

## Email
Write to support@example.com from the email address on your account. Include the order ID.

## Complaints
If you're unhappy with how we handled something, reply to the ticket email and ask for it to be escalated. A team
lead will review it.
