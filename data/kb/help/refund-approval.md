# Large refunds and approval

Refunds of ₹5,000 or less are released automatically once a return is checked or a prepaid order is cancelled.

Any refund above ₹5,000 needs human approval from our support team before it is released. This is a safety check
against fraud on high-value orders; it doesn't mean anything is wrong with your return.

## What this means for you
- Our chat assistant can't release a refund above ₹5,000 by itself. It will raise a ticket for the support team.
- A member of the team reviews the order, usually within one business day, and then issues the refund to the
  original payment method.
- You'll get an email when the refund is issued.

## Examples
- A Robot Vacuum Lite returned for ₹14,899 is reviewed by the team before the refund is issued.
- A pair of TWS Earbuds Pro returned for ₹2,499 is refunded automatically.

Nobody from SaffronCart will ever ask for your UPI PIN, card PIN or an OTP to "speed up" a refund. See
[Privacy and your data](privacy-and-data).
