# Mixer Grinder 750W

A 750-watt mixer grinder for chutneys, masalas, idli and dosa batter and juices.

## Prices
- With 3 jars: ₹3,399
- With 4 jars: ₹3,499

The 4-jar version adds a juicer jar with a strainer.

## Using it well
- Don't fill a jar more than two-thirds full, and add a little water when grinding wet batter.
- Run it in short bursts of up to 1 minute, then rest it for a minute. Long runs make the motor overheat.
- If the motor stops suddenly, the overload protector has tripped. Unplug it, wait 10 minutes and press the reset
  button underneath.

## Cleaning
Wash jars with warm water and mild soap. Don't put the jars in a dishwasher or immerse the motor base in water.

Returnable within 10 days of delivery with all jars and lids. 1-year warranty from the delivery date.
