# Privacy and your data

## What we keep
Your name, email address, phone number, delivery addresses, order history, saved UPI ID and the last four digits of
any saved card. We need these to deliver orders, process refunds and help you when you contact support.

## What we never ask for
Nobody from SaffronCart, including our chat assistant, will ever ask for:
- your UPI PIN, card PIN or CVV
- an OTP sent to your phone, except inside the SaffronCart app or website
- remote access to your phone or computer

If someone claiming to be from SaffronCart asks for these, it's a scam. Don't share anything, and report it to us.

## Who we share data with
Only the partners needed to complete your order: the courier (name, phone and delivery address), the payment gateway
(payment details) and, for warranty claims, the authorised service centre. We don't sell your data.

## Our chat assistant
The assistant can look up your own orders after you confirm your email address and order ID. It can't see other
customers' orders, and it won't send your details to any email address or website other than your own.

## Your rights
You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account. Write to
privacy@example.com. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 we respond to these requests promptly.
