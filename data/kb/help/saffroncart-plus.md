# SaffronCart Plus

SaffronCart Plus is our membership for frequent shoppers. Your account page shows "Plus" next to your name if
you're a member.

## What Plus members get
- Priority support: your chats and tickets go to the front of the queue.
- Early access to sales.

## What stays the same
Plus doesn't change our policies. Returns still close 10 days after delivery, non-returnable items stay
non-returnable, and refunds above ₹5,000 still need approval from the support team like everyone else's.
