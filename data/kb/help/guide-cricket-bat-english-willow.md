# Cricket Bat English Willow

A full-size English willow bat for leather-ball cricket.

## Prices
- Short handle (SH): ₹7,399
- Long handle (LH): ₹7,499

Most adults up to about six feet tall use a short handle.

## Before you play
English willow needs knocking-in before it meets a new leather ball:
1. Apply a thin coat of raw linseed oil to the face and edges, and let it dry for a day.
2. Knock the face and edges with an old ball or a bat mallet for 4 to 6 hours over several days.
3. Play a few net sessions with older balls before a match.

## Care
Keep it out of direct sun and away from damp. Small surface cracks on the face are normal for willow.
