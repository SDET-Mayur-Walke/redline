# TWS Earbuds Pro

True wireless earbuds with a pocket charging case.

## Prices
- Black: ₹2,599
- White: ₹2,499
- Blue: ₹2,499

## Getting started
1. Open the case lid near your phone. The earbuds enter pairing mode automatically the first time.
2. Choose "TWS Earbuds Pro" in your phone's Bluetooth settings.
3. To pair a new phone, hold the button on the case for 5 seconds until the light blinks white.

## Tips
- If only one earbud plays, put both back in the case, close the lid for 10 seconds and take them out again.
- Clean the charging contacts with a dry cotton bud if an earbud stops charging.
