# Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order only while its status is **Pending**, which means it hasn't left our warehouse yet.

1. Open **My Orders** and choose the order.
2. Tap **Cancel order** and pick a reason.

The whole order is cancelled; we can't cancel a single item from a pending order. If you only want to drop one item,
cancel the order and place a new one with the items you want.

## Refunds for cancelled orders
- Paid by UPI or card: the full amount is refunded to the same UPI ID or card. See
  [Refund timelines](refund-timelines).
- Cash on Delivery: nothing was paid, so there's nothing to refund.

## The order has already shipped
Once an order is **Shipped** it can't be cancelled. You can refuse the parcel at the door, or accept it and return it
within 10 days of delivery under our [returns policy](returns-policy).
