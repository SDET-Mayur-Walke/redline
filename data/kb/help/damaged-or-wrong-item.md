# Damaged, defective or wrong item

We're sorry when an order arrives in bad shape. Please report it within 10 days of delivery so we can sort it out
quickly. This applies to every product, including ones that are normally non-returnable.

## How to report it
1. Open the order in **My Orders** and tap **Return item**.
2. Choose "Damaged", "Defective" or "Wrong item received".
3. Add clear photos: the product, the label on the outer box and any visible damage.

We'll arrange a free pickup and then refund the item or send a replacement, whichever you choose. If the same
product is out of stock, we refund it.

## Missing items
If an order arrives with an item missing, don't open a return. Contact us through
[Contacting support](contact-support) and we'll raise a ticket with the courier.

## After the 10-day window
Faults that show up later are handled under the manufacturer warranty. See [Warranty and repairs](warranty).
