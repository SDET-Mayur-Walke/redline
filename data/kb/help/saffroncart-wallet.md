# SaffronCart Wallet

Your SaffronCart Wallet holds money we've credited to you: refunds for Cash on Delivery orders, refunds you chose to
take as wallet credit, and goodwill credits from our support team.

## Checking your balance
Go to **Account > Wallet**. Every credit shows the order it came from.

## Using your balance
Wallet balance is applied at checkout before you choose how to pay the rest. It doesn't expire.

## Moving wallet money to your bank
Wallet balance can't be withdrawn to a bank account or transferred to another SaffronCart customer. If a COD refund
landed in your wallet and you'd rather have it on UPI, contact support within 10 days of the refund and we can move
it to a UPI ID saved on your account.

## Wallet and fraud
Nobody from SaffronCart will ask you to "load" your wallet or pay a fee to unlock a refund.
