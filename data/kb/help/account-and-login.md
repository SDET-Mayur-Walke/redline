# Your account and signing in

## Signing in
You sign in with your email address or phone number and a one-time password (OTP). We don't use passwords.

## Changing your email or phone number
Go to **Account > Profile**. We send an OTP to both the old and the new contact before the change goes through. If
you've lost access to the old number, contact support; we'll verify your identity using your recent orders before
updating it.

## Who can see your orders
Only you. Our support team will only discuss an order with the account holder, after confirming the email address
on the account and the order ID. We won't share order details, addresses or payment information with anyone else,
even family members, without that verification.

## Closing your account
Contact support to close your account. Open orders and pending refunds must be completed first. See
[Privacy and your data](privacy-and-data).
