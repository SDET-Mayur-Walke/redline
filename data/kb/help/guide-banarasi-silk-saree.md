# Banarasi Silk Saree

A handwoven silk saree with zari work, 6.3 metres including an unstitched blouse piece.

## Prices
- Red: ₹6,599
- Green: ₹6,399
- Gold: ₹6,499

## Care
- Dry clean only for the first wash.
- Store it wrapped in a muslin cloth, not plastic, and refold it along different lines every few months so the zari
  doesn't crack.
- Iron on low heat from the reverse side.

## Handwoven variation
Small irregularities in the weave and slight colour differences between pieces are part of the handloom process and
aren't defects.

## Returns
Returnable within 10 days of delivery, unworn, with the tag attached and the blouse piece uncut. Because a refund for
this item is above ₹5,000, it needs approval from our support team after the return is checked. Sarees have no
warranty.
