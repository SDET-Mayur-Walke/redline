# Vitamin C Serum

A lightweight vitamin C serum for daily use, in a 30ml dropper bottle.

## Price
- 30ml: ₹799

## How to use
1. Do a patch test on your inner arm 24 hours before first use.
2. Apply 3 to 4 drops to clean, dry skin in the morning.
3. Follow with moisturiser and sunscreen.

## Storage
Keep it in a cool, dark place with the cap tightly closed. The serum may darken slightly over time; that's oxidation
and is expected.
