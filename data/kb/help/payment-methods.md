# Ways to pay

SaffronCart accepts:

- **UPI**: pay from any UPI app using your UPI ID. See [UPI payment failed](upi-payment-failures).
- **Debit and credit cards**: Visa, Mastercard and RuPay.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: pay the courier in cash or by UPI QR code at the door. See
  [Cash on Delivery](cash-on-delivery).

Your **SaffronCart Wallet** holds refunds and credits. See [SaffronCart Wallet](saffroncart-wallet).

## Saved payment methods
You can save a UPI ID and a card on your account to check out faster. We only store the last four digits of a card;
the full number stays with the payment gateway. To remove a saved method, go to **Account > Payments**.

## No hidden charges
The price you see at checkout is the price you pay. Delivery is free and there is no extra fee for COD.
