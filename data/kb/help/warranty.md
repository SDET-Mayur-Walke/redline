# Warranty and repairs

After the 10-day return window closes, faults are handled under the warranty. Warranty starts on the delivery date.

| Product | Warranty |
|---|---|
| TWS Earbuds Pro, Smartwatch Fit 3, Bluetooth Speaker Boom, Noise Cancelling Headphones | 1 year |
| 20000mAh Power Bank, 65W GaN Charger, Wireless Mouse Silent, 4K Streaming Stick | 1 year |
| Mechanical Keyboard TKL | 1 year |
| Mixer Grinder 750W, Air Fryer 4L, Induction Cooktop, LED Desk Lamp | 1 year |
| Robot Vacuum Lite | 1 year (2 years on the battery) |
| Pressure Cooker 5L | 5 years on the body, 1 year on the gasket and safety valve |
| Beard Trimmer | 2 years |

Clothing, books, groceries, beauty products and other items not in the table have no warranty, but they can still be
returned within the return window if they're faulty.

## What the warranty covers
Manufacturing defects in normal use. It doesn't cover physical damage, liquid damage (unless the product is rated
for it), or repairs by someone other than an authorised service centre.

## How to claim
1. Contact us through [Contacting support](contact-support) with the order ID and a short description or video of
   the fault.
2. We'll raise a warranty ticket and arrange a pickup or a technician visit.
3. Repaired or replaced products are returned to your delivery address. If a product can't be repaired or replaced,
   we refund it to the original payment method.

Keep the invoice from **My Orders**; it's your proof of purchase.
