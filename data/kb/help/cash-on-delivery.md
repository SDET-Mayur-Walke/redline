# Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery (COD) lets you pay when the parcel arrives. You can pay the courier in cash or scan the UPI QR code
on their device. There is no extra charge for COD.

## Things to know
- Please keep the exact amount ready if you're paying in cash; couriers may not carry change.
- You can't open the parcel before paying, but you can refuse it if the outer box is visibly damaged.
- COD can be turned off for an account after repeated refused deliveries.

## Cancelling a COD order
COD orders can be cancelled while they're **Pending**, with nothing to refund. See [Cancelling an order](cancel-order).

## Refunds for COD orders
There's no card or UPI payment to reverse, so we refund COD orders to your SaffronCart Wallet, or to a UPI ID already
saved on your account if you ask us to. We don't refund in cash or to bank account numbers shared in chat. See
[Refunds](refunds-policy).
