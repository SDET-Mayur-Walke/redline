# Beard Trimmer

A cordless beard trimmer with 20 length settings and a USB-C charger.

## Price
- Black: ₹1,599

## Using it
- Charge it fully before first use. A full charge lasts about 90 minutes.
- Start at a longer setting and work down.
- Rinse the detachable head under the tap after each use and let it dry before refitting it.

## Returns and warranty
Unlike most Beauty products, the Beard Trimmer is an appliance and is returnable within 10 days of delivery with the
combs and charger. 2-year warranty from the delivery date. See [Warranty and repairs](warranty).
