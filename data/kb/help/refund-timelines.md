# Refund timelines

The clock starts when your refund is issued: after the returned item passes its check, or straight away for a
cancelled prepaid order.

| Refund to | Usually arrives in |
|---|---|
| SaffronCart Wallet | Instantly |
| UPI | 1 to 3 business days |
| Debit or credit card | 5 to 7 business days |

Banks sometimes take longer around public holidays. If a UPI refund hasn't arrived after 3 business days, or a card
refund after 7, contact us with the order ID and we'll share the refund reference number so your bank can trace it.

## Partial refunds
If you return one item from an order with several, only that item is refunded. The order details show each refund
separately.

## Large refunds
A refund above ₹5,000 waits for approval by our support team before it is issued, which can add up to one business
day. See [Large refunds and approval](refund-approval).
