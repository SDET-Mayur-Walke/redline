# Robot Vacuum Lite

A robot vacuum with dry and wet mopping, app scheduling and automatic return to its dock.

## Price
- Black: ₹14,899

## Setting it up
1. Place the dock against a wall with half a metre of clear space on each side.
2. Charge the robot on the dock for 4 hours before the first clean.
3. Connect it to your home Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz only) from the companion app.

## Before each clean
Pick up loose cables, socks and anything lighter than a slipper. Close doors to rooms you don't want cleaned.

## Mopping
Fill the water tank with plain water only. Floor cleaners and phenyl damage the pump.
