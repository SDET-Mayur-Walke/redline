# Noise Cancelling Headphones

Over-ear wireless headphones with active noise cancellation (ANC) and a transparency mode.

## Prices
- Black: ₹9,199
- Beige: ₹9,099

## Getting started
1. Hold the power button for 2 seconds to turn them on. They enter pairing mode on first use.
2. Press the ANC button once for noise cancellation, again for transparency mode, and again to turn both off.
3. Use the included 3.5mm cable on flights; the headphones still work as wired headphones when the battery is flat.

## Tips
- A faint hiss with ANC on and no music playing is normal.
- If the ear cushions feel tight at first, they loosen within a week.
