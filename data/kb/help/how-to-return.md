# How to return an item

1. Open **My Orders** in the app or on the website and choose the delivered order.
2. Tap **Return item**. The button only shows while the order is within 10 days of delivery.
3. Choose the item and a reason. For a damaged or wrong item, add a photo of the product and the outer box.
4. Pick a pickup slot. Our courier partner collects returns from the delivery address at no charge.
5. Pack the item with all accessories and hand it over. Keep the pickup receipt the courier gives you.

Once the item reaches our warehouse and passes a quick check, the order status changes to **Returned** and your
refund starts. Most checks finish within 2 business days of pickup.

## Can I return just one item from an order?
Yes. Each item in an order can be returned separately, and you're refunded for that item's price only.

## I can't see the Return button
- The 10-day return window may have closed. Check the delivery date in your order details.
- The item may be non-returnable. See [Items that can't be returned](non-returnable-items).
- The order may still be in transit. Returns open only after delivery.

If none of these apply, contact us through [Contacting support](contact-support).
