# Air Fryer 4L

A 4-litre air fryer that cooks with hot air and little or no oil. Good for samosas, tikkis, fries and paneer tikka.

## Prices
- Black: ₹6,099
- White: ₹6,199

## First use
Run it empty at 200°C for 10 minutes to burn off the factory coating. A light smell the first time is normal.

## Cooking tips
- Don't overfill the basket; food cooks evenly in a single layer.
- Shake the basket halfway through.
- Brush a little oil on frozen snacks for a crisper finish.
