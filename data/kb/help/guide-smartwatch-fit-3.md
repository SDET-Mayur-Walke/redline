# Smartwatch Fit 3

A fitness smartwatch with heart-rate tracking, sleep tracking and call notifications.

## Prices
- Silicone strap, black: ₹4,099
- Silicone strap, rose gold: ₹3,999
- Metal strap, black: ₹3,899
- Metal strap, rose gold: ₹4,099

## Getting started
1. Charge the watch fully on the magnetic charger before first use.
2. Install the companion app, turn on Bluetooth and follow the pairing steps in the app.
3. Allow notification access in your phone's settings so messages and calls show on the watch.

## Water resistance
Fine for hand-washing and rain. Don't wear it in a sauna, steam room or hot shower; heat can damage the seals.

## Returns and warranty
Returnable within 10 days of delivery with the charger and both straps. 1-year warranty from the delivery date.
Strap wear and scratches on the screen aren't covered. See [Warranty and repairs](warranty).
