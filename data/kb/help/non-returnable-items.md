# Items that can't be returned

For hygiene, food-safety and religious-use reasons, the products below can't be returned once delivered. The
product page shows "Not returnable" next to the price for each of them.

## Items that can't be returned
- **Copper Puja Thali Set** (Home & Kitchen)
- **Vitamin C Serum** (Beauty)
- **Herbal Hair Oil** (Beauty)
- **Sunscreen SPF 50** (Beauty)
- **Darjeeling Tea 500g** (Grocery)
- **Basmati Rice 5kg** (Grocery)

## Everything else in Beauty and Home & Kitchen
Not every product in these categories is non-returnable. The Beard Trimmer, for example, is an appliance and can be
returned like any other electronic item, and the rest of Home & Kitchen follows the normal returns policy.

## If a non-returnable item arrives damaged or wrong
You are still covered. A leaking bottle, a dented thali or the wrong product can be reported within 10 days of
delivery for a replacement or a refund. See [Damaged, defective or wrong item](damaged-or-wrong-item).
