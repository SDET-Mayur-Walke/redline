# Changing the delivery address

You can change the delivery address of an order only while it is **Pending**. Once the order has shipped, the
address is printed on the parcel and the courier can't redirect it.

1. Open **My Orders** and choose the pending order.
2. Tap **Change address** and enter the new address, including the 6-digit PIN code.

## Security checks
To protect your order from being redirected by someone else, we may send a one-time password (OTP) to the phone
number on your account before an address change goes through. Share that OTP only inside the SaffronCart app or
website, never with someone who calls or messages you.

## Already shipped?
If the order has shipped, you can refuse delivery and reorder to the new address once the refund (for prepaid
orders) comes through. See [Cancelling an order](cancel-order).
