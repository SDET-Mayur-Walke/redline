# Sizes, colours and exchanges

SaffronCart doesn't offer direct exchanges. To get a different size or colour:

1. Return the original item within 10 days of delivery (see [How to return an item](how-to-return)).
2. Place a new order for the size or colour you want.

Placing the new order first means you don't risk the size selling out while your return is picked up.

## Size tips
- **Cotton Kurta**: sizes S to XL. If you're between sizes, choose the larger one; the cotton shrinks a little in
  the first wash.
- **Running Sneakers**: UK sizes 7 to 10. They run true to size.
- **Denim Jeans Slim**: waist 30 to 36 inches. This is a slim fit; size up for a relaxed fit.
- **Kolhapuri Chappals**: UK sizes 6 to 9. Leather softens and stretches slightly with wear.

## Colour differences
Colours can look slightly different on screen. Handloom and hand-dyed products, like the Banarasi Silk Saree and the
Embroidered Dupatta, can vary a little from piece to piece. That isn't treated as a defect, but these items can still
be returned under the normal returns policy.
