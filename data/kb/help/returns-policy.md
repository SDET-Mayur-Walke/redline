# Our returns policy

Most items bought on SaffronCart can be returned within 10 days of delivery. The 10-day return window starts on
the date and time your order is marked "Delivered" in **My Orders**, not the date you placed it.

## What you can return
- Items in their original condition, with tags, manuals, accessories and free gifts included.
- Electronics in their original box, with the seal on the accessories pouch unbroken where there is one.
- Clothing and footwear that has not been worn outside, washed or altered.

## What you can't return
A small number of products can't be returned once delivered, usually for hygiene or food-safety reasons. They are
listed in [Items that can't be returned](non-returnable-items). If one of these arrives damaged or wrong, you can
still report it: see [Damaged, defective or wrong item](damaged-or-wrong-item).

## After the window closes
Once the 10-day return window has passed we can't accept a return, but products covered by a manufacturer warranty
can still be repaired or replaced. See [Warranty and repairs](warranty).

## Exchanges
We don't do direct exchanges yet. If you need a different size or colour, return the item and place a new order.
See [Sizes, colours and exchanges](exchanges-and-sizes).

## Your refund
Refunds go back to the method you paid with. Orders paid by Cash on Delivery are refunded to your SaffronCart Wallet
or to a UPI ID. Full details are in [Refunds](refunds-policy).
