# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery charges
Delivery is free on every SaffronCart order, with no minimum order value. There's also no extra fee for Cash on
Delivery.

## How long delivery takes
Most orders are delivered 2 to 6 days after they're placed. Metro cities are usually at the quicker end; smaller
towns and remote PIN codes take longer.

We currently deliver to these cities and their surrounding PIN codes: Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur, Bengaluru, Mysuru,
Chennai, Coimbatore, Hyderabad, Kochi, New Delhi, Gurugram, Jaipur, Ahmedabad, Kolkata, Lucknow, Chandigarh,
Indore and Bhubaneswar.

## Who delivers
Orders are delivered by our courier partner. The tracking number appears in your order details as soon as the
order ships. See [Tracking your order](track-order).

## Delivery attempts
The courier calls the phone number on your account before delivering. If nobody is available, they try again the
next working day. Large appliances, like the Robot Vacuum Lite, need a signature on delivery.
