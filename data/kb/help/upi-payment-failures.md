# UPI payment failed or money deducted

## The payment failed and no money left my account
Nothing to worry about. Go back to your cart and try again, or choose a different payment method. Common causes:
- The collect request expired before you approved it in your UPI app.
- Your bank's UPI server was busy. Waiting a few minutes usually helps.
- You hit your bank's daily UPI limit.

## Money was deducted but the order wasn't placed
This happens when the bank debits you but the confirmation doesn't reach us. You don't need to do anything: the bank
reverses the debit automatically, usually within 3 business days. If it hasn't come back after that, contact us with
the UPI transaction reference (the 12-digit number in your UPI app) and we'll raise it with the payment gateway.

## Money was deducted twice
If you were charged twice for one order, the extra debit is reversed automatically within 3 business days. If it
isn't, contact us with both UPI references.

## Stay safe
SaffronCart will never send you a UPI collect request to "receive" a refund, and never asks for your UPI PIN. Entering
your PIN always sends money out of your account. See [Privacy and your data](privacy-and-data).
