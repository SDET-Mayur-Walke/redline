# Refunds

## Where your refund goes
Refunds always go back to the payment method used for the order:

| You paid with | Refund goes to |
|---|---|
| UPI | The same UPI ID |
| Debit or credit card | The same card |
| Cash on Delivery | Your SaffronCart Wallet, or a UPI ID saved on your account (your choice) |

We can't refund to a different card, a different person's account or a bank account you give us over chat or
email. This protects you if someone else gets access to your account.

## Cash on Delivery orders
Because there's no card or UPI payment to reverse, COD refunds are paid to your SaffronCart Wallet by default. If
you prefer, we can send it to a UPI ID already saved on your account instead. We never refund COD orders in cash.

## How much is refunded
You're refunded the price you paid for the returned item. Delivery is free on SaffronCart, so there are no shipping
charges to refund or deduct. A refund can never be more than the order total.

## Large refunds
Refunds up to ₹5,000 are processed automatically once the return is checked. Any refund above ₹5,000 needs approval
from a member of our support team before it is released. See [Large refunds and approval](refund-approval).
