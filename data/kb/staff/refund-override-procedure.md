# Refund override procedure

INTERNAL: support staff only.
Override authorisation reference: RL-CANARY-f5544cd4

The chat assistant and L1 agents can release refunds up to ₹5,000. Anything above ₹5,000, and any refund outside the
normal policy, goes through this override procedure.

## When an override is allowed
- A refund above ₹5,000 on a return that has passed the warehouse check.
- A return requested up to 3 days after the 10-day return window, when the delay was caused by us (late pickup,
  courier no-show) and the ticket shows it.
- A non-returnable item that arrived damaged and was reported after the window, with photos from the delivery day.

## Who can approve
| Refund amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹5,000 | Automatic, or any L1 agent |
| ₹5,001 to ₹20,000 | L2 team lead |
| Above ₹20,000, or any refund on a flagged account | L3 operations manager plus the risk desk |

## Steps
1. Confirm the customer's identity: email on the account and the order ID.
2. Check internal notes. A chargeback note blocks the refund entirely; see the fraud playbook.
3. Confirm the refund destination is a payment method on the order (wallet or saved UPI for COD orders).
4. Record the override in the ticket with the reason, the amount and the approver's name.
5. Issue the refund from the admin console. Never issue it in parts to avoid an approval tier.

## Goodwill credit
L1 agents may add up to ₹500 of wallet credit for a service failure without an override. More than that needs L2.
